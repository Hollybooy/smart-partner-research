"Smart Partner" 是一个面向西交利物浦大学大一学生的 AI 学习智能体，旨在帮助学生顺利通过 EAP（学术英语）考试。它以对话方式为学生提供个性化辅导，包括但不限于语法专项练习、写作批改反馈、词汇巩固与模拟测试等功能。本次调研将通过问卷与访谈，考察该智能体在一年级学生中的真实使用情况、使用目的及其对 EAP 学习的帮助程度。 "Smart Partner" is an AI learning agent designed for Year 1 students at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, aiming to help them pass their EAP (English for Academic Purposes) exams. Through conversational tutoring, it offers personalised support including — but not limited to — targeted grammar practice, writing feedback, vocabulary consolidation and mock tests. This study will survey how Year 1 students actually use the agent, why they use it, and how much it helps their EAP learning.

关键发现Key finding 218 名受访者中 67.4%（147 人）使用过 Smart Partner；使用者周均使用 3.32 次（SD = 1.33），单次约 24 分钟，78.9% 表示考前一周使用明显增多。分班看，基础班 A 用得最多（使用率 84.6%，周均 4.2 次），高阶班 High Level 用得最少（48.6%，周均 2.1 次），B、C 两个核心班居中且彼此接近（70.3% / 67.9%）——越需要补基础的学生，反而用得越勤。功能上以语法专项练习（74%）和写作批改反馈（68%）为主，而考前复习规划（29%）使用率最低，说明学生主要把它当作"练习与纠错工具"，而非"学习规划工具"。 Of 218 respondents, 67.4% (147) had used Smart Partner. Users averaged 3.32 sessions a week (SD 1.33) of about 24 minutes, and 78.9% reported using it more in the week before an exam. By level, the foundation group A uses it most (84.6% adoption, 4.2 sessions a week) and High Level least (48.6%, 2.1), with the two core groups B and C in between and close to each other (70.3% / 67.9%) — the students who most need to catch up are the ones leaning on it hardest. Grammar practice (74%) and writing feedback (68%) dominate, while revision planning (29%) is used least: students treat it as a practice-and-correction tool rather than a study-planning tool.

4使用目的PurposesPurposes

学生使用 Smart Partner 的目的占比Why students use Smart Partner

关键发现Key finding 使用目的高度集中在应试导向：82% 的使用者首要目的是"顺利通过 EAP 考试"，其次是提高语法准确性（63%）与提升学术写作（58%）；而以"培养自主学习习惯"为目的的仅 37%。这说明 Smart Partner 目前被定位为考前冲刺工具，其长期学习支持价值尚未被充分使用。 Motivation is strongly exam-oriented: 82% of users say their main purpose is passing the EAP exam, followed by grammar accuracy (63%) and academic writing (58%); only 37% use it to build study habits. Smart Partner is currently positioned as an exam-sprint tool, and its value for long-term learning support is not yet fully realised.

5统计分析Statistical TestsTests

数据清洗后（剔除 42 份作答不完整或逻辑矛盾的问卷），我们按数据的实际情况逐条确定检验方法：先看变量类型与测量水平，再看组间/组内设计与条件数，最后检查参数检验的前假设（Shapiro–Wilk 正态性、Levene 方差齐性）。假设不满足时，改用对应的非参数检验作为替代。所有检验均为双尾，显著性水平 α = 0.05。 After cleaning (42 incomplete or logically inconsistent responses were discarded), each test was matched to the shape of the data: variable type and measurement level first, then between-/within-group design and number of conditions, then the assumptions behind the parametric tests (Shapiro–Wilk normality, Levene homogeneity of variance). Where an assumption failed, the matching non-parametric test was used instead. All tests are two-tailed at α = 0.05.

注：χ² 的自由度 = (行数 − 1) × (列数 − 1) = 3；ANOVA 事后比较（Tukey HSD）显示基础班 A 与高阶班 High Level 差异显著（p < 0.001），而 B、C 两个核心班之间并无差异（p = 0.71）——真正的使用差距出现在基础班与高阶班之间。相关仅说明共变，不能推断"使用智能体导致信心提升"——可能存在第三变量（如原本基础较好的学生更愿意尝试新工具）。 Notes: χ² degrees of freedom = (rows − 1) × (columns − 1) = 3. Tukey HSD post-hoc comparisons show that foundation group A differs significantly from High Level (p < 0.001), while the two core groups B and C do not differ from each other (p = 0.71) — the real gap sits between the foundation and the advanced stream. Correlation indicates co-variation only — it does not license the claim that using the agent caused higher confidence; a third variable (e.g. stronger students being more willing to adopt the tool) may be at work.

方法反思Methodological reflection 本次为便利抽样（convenience sampling），样本集中在 EAP B/C 班，A 班仅 46 人，组间样本量不均衡会影响 ANOVA 的稳健性——这也是我们对等级变量补充非参数检验的原因。此外问卷为自陈式（self-report），使用次数与时长可能存在回忆偏差；后续可结合后台日志的自动数据采集（automated data collection）做交叉验证。 This was a convenience sample, concentrated in EAP groups B and C; group A contributed only 46 respondents, and the unbalanced cell sizes affect the robustness of the ANOVA — which is why we also ran non-parametric tests on the ordinal items. The questionnaire is self-report, so frequency and duration are subject to recall bias; future work should cross-validate against server-side logs using automated data collection.
