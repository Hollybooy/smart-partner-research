01Research questions

我们评估的不是"喜不喜欢"，而是"改没改对"We measure whether drafts improve, not whether users approve

"Smart Partner" 是面向西交利物浦大学大一学生的 AI 学习智能体，通过对话提供语法专项练习、写作批改反馈、词汇巩固与模拟测试，目标是帮助学生通过 EAP（学术英语）考试。市面上对这类工具的评价大多停留在"你觉得有用吗"，但满意度不等于学习效果：学生可能高度认可一个只替他改错、却不促使他思考的工具。因此本研究让参与者先完成一个真实的学习任务，再用任务表现来评估效果。 "Smart Partner" is an AI learning agent for Year 1 students at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, offering targeted grammar practice, writing feedback, vocabulary consolidation and mock tests, with the goal of helping students pass their EAP (English for Academic Purposes) exams. Evaluation of such tools usually stops at "did you find it useful" — but satisfaction is not learning: students may rate highly a tool that silently fixes their errors without making them think. This study therefore asks participants to complete a real learning task first, and judges effectiveness by what their work looks like afterwards.

为什么不能只发一份问卷Why a questionnaire alone is not an evaluation

把问卷当作主要证据有四个无法修复的缺陷：① 没有客观锚点——"你觉得进步了吗"无法与"你的草稿实际变好了多少"互相校验；② 无法分离效应来源——学生报告的提升可能来自练习本身、来自时间推移，而非 AI 的反馈方式；③ 需求表述与有效设计脱节——学生说"最想要"的功能，往往不是真正促成修订的功能；④ 回忆偏差——自陈的使用频率与时长系统性失真。本研究的处理方式：问卷降级为任务后的辅助工具，用于解释客观结果的成因（为什么某一条件下提升更大、认知负荷更高），而非作为效果证据本身。 Using questionnaires as primary evidence has four irreparable flaws: (i) no objective anchor — "do you feel you improved" cannot be checked against "how much your draft actually improved"; (ii) effects cannot be attributed — reported gains may come from practice itself or the passage of time, not from the AI's feedback style; (iii) stated wants diverge from effective design — the feature students say they most want is often not the one that drives revision; (iv) recall bias — self-reported frequency and duration are systematically distorted. Our response: the questionnaire is demoted to a post-task supplementary instrument, used to explain the objective results (why one condition produced larger gains or heavier load), never as the evidence of effectiveness itself.

研究问题Research questions
  1. 主效应（被试间）：接受直接纠错与引导提示的参与者，修订后的草稿质量是否存在差异？Between-group main effect — Does revised draft quality differ between participants receiving direct correction and guided scaffolding?
  2. 主效应（被试内）：不论条件如何，参与者修订后的草稿质量相比初稿是否显著提升？Within-group main effect — Regardless of condition, does draft quality improve significantly from first draft to revision?
  3. 交互作用（核心）：反馈方式是否调节修订带来的提升幅度——即"反馈方式 × 时间"是否存在交互？Interaction (core) — Does feedback style moderate the size of the gain, i.e. is there a feedback × time interaction?
  4. 过程机制：修订行为（交互轮次、建议采纳率、自主修正数）能否预测提升幅度？Process mechanism — Do revision behaviours (turns, adoption rate, self-corrections) predict the size of the gain?
  5. 一致性检验：参与者的主观评价（有用性、信任、认知负荷）与客观修订质量是否一致？Correspondence check — Do subjective ratings (usefulness, trust, cognitive load) agree with objective revision quality?
02Evaluation design

2×2 混合设计A 2 × 2 mixed design

本研究采用混合因子设计（mixed / split-plot design），同时包含一个被试间因子与一个被试内因子。这样做的好处是：既能比较两种 AI 反馈方式的相对优劣，又能确认每位参与者自身是否真的进步，并且可以检验"哪种方式让人进步更多"这一最有设计价值的问题。 This study uses a mixed (split-plot) factorial design containing one between-subjects factor and one within-subjects factor. The advantage is threefold: we can compare the relative merit of two AI feedback styles, confirm whether each participant individually improved, and test the question with the greatest design value — which style produces the larger gain.

被试间因子（Feedback style）：参与者被随机分配到 A（直接纠错）或 B（引导提示），每人只接受一种——避免同一人先后体验两种反馈带来的顺序效应与需求特征。
被试内因子（Time）：所有参与者都经历 T1（初稿）与 T2（终稿）两个测量时点，每个人都是自己的对照，从而抵消个体写作水平差异这一最大的混淆变量。
分层变量（EAP level）：A / B / C / High Level 作为分层依据参与随机化，保证两个条件在各层级上的分布均衡。 Between-subjects factor (Feedback style) — participants are randomised to A (direct correction) or B (guided scaffolding) and receive only one, avoiding order effects and demand characteristics from experiencing both.
Within-subjects factor (Time) — all participants are measured at T1 (first draft) and T2 (final draft), so each person serves as their own control, cancelling out individual writing proficiency, the largest confound.
Stratifying variable (EAP level) — A / B / C / High Level is used to stratify randomisation so that the two conditions stay balanced across levels.

随机化与盲法Randomisation and blinding
  1. 分层区组随机化：按 EAP 层级分层，层内以 4 为区组长度随机分配 A / B，由不参与施测的成员生成分配序列。Stratified block randomisation — stratify by EAP level, randomise A/B within stratum using blocks of 4, with the sequence generated by a member not involved in data collection.
  2. 评分者盲法：终稿去除条件标识与交互记录后送评，两名评分者独立打分，不知其所属条件。Rater blinding — final drafts are stripped of condition labels and interaction logs before scoring; two raters score independently without knowing the condition.
  3. 评分者一致性：以 ICC(2,2) 检验，目标 > 0.75；不一致条目由第三人仲裁。Inter-rater reliability — assessed with ICC(2,2), target > 0.75; disagreements are adjudicated by a third rater.
  4. 任务材料固定：所有参与者使用同一写作题目、同一时间限制与同一评分量表，仅反馈方式不同。Held-constant materials — all participants use the same prompt, the same time limit and the same scoring rubric; only the feedback style differs.
03The task

参与者具体要做什么What participants actually do

任务为一次完整的 EAP 段落写作与修订，总时长约 35 分钟，分三个阶段连续进行。任务来自真实的 EAP 训练场景——写一段议论性文字并根据反馈修改——因此参与者的行为就是他们平时备考时的行为，而非针对实验设计的表演。 The task is one complete cycle of EAP paragraph writing and revision, lasting about 35 minutes and run continuously in three stages. It is drawn from a genuine EAP practice scenario — write an argumentative paragraph, then revise it in response to feedback — so what participants do is what they would normally do while preparing for the exam, rather than a performance staged for the study.

阶段 1 · 前测写作（10 分钟，无 AI）Stage 1 · Baseline writing (10 min, no AI)
  1. 阅读固定写作题目（argumentative prompt，见下方材料）。Read the fixed writing prompt (argumentative; see materials below).
  2. 在 10 分钟内独立写出 150–180 词的议论段落，不允许使用任何 AI 工具或词典。Independently write a 150–180-word argumentative paragraph within 10 minutes, without any AI tool or dictionary.
  3. 提交初稿，并立即填写前测自评（信心 1–10、任务难度感知）。Submit the first draft and immediately complete the pre-test self-ratings (confidence 1–10, perceived task difficulty).
阶段 2 · AI 辅助修订（15 分钟）Stage 2 · AI-assisted revision (15 min)
  1. 研究者按预先生成的随机序列，为该参与者启用对应条件的 Smart Partner 会话。Following the pre-generated randomisation sequence, the researcher opens a Smart Partner session in the participant's assigned condition.
  2. 参与者将自己的初稿提交给 Smart Partner 请求反馈，并据此修订。交互过程全程记录：轮次、时长、AI 给出的建议条目、实际被写入终稿的条目、参与者自己发现并修正的错误。The participant submits their first draft to Smart Partner for feedback and revises accordingly. The entire interaction is logged: turns, duration, items of AI advice, items actually adopted into the final draft, and errors the participant detected and fixed unaided.
  3. 研究者不介入、不提示，仅在时间结束时收取终稿。The researcher does not intervene or prompt, and simply collects the final draft when time expires.
阶段 3 · 后测与延迟迁移Stage 3 · Post-test and delayed transfer
  1. 即时后测（5 分钟）：终稿提交后填写感知有用性、信任、认知负荷与后测信心。Immediate post-test (5 min) — after submitting the final draft, complete perceived usefulness, trust, cognitive load and post-test confidence.
  2. 延迟迁移测验（1 周后，10 分钟）：使用同类型的新题目再写一段，全程无 AI。这一阶段检验的不是"当次稿子改好没有"，而是"学到的东西还在不在"——这是区分两类反馈的关键。Delayed transfer test (1 week later, 10 min) — write another paragraph on a new prompt of the same type, with no AI available. This stage tests not "was this draft fixed" but "does the learning persist" — the outcome on which the two feedback styles are expected to diverge.

评分量表Scoring rubric

采用 EAP 段落评分维度：语言准确性（0–40）、论证结构与衔接（0–30）、学术语体恰当性（0–30），合计 0–100。两名经训练的评分者独立打分，取均值。EAP paragraph criteria: language accuracy (0–40), argument structure and cohesion (0–30), appropriateness of academic register (0–30); total 0–100. Two trained raters score independently and the mean is used.

04Intervention

两种反馈方式The two feedback styles

干预就是 Smart Partner 回复草稿的方式。两个条件的反馈内容量相同（都指出全部错误），差别只在于是否直接给出正确形式。这一对照直接对应产品决策：AI 应该替用户改，还是逼用户自己想。 The intervention is simply how Smart Partner replies to the draft. Both conditions flag the same set of errors; the only difference is whether the correct form is supplied. This contrast maps directly onto a product decision: should the AI fix things for the user, or make the user work it out?

条件 ACondition A

直接纠错Direct correction

直接给出修改后的文本，并逐条说明错误类型与正确形式。参与者只需照做即可完成修订。Supplies the corrected text along with an itemised list of error types and correct forms. The participant can complete the revision by simply applying them.

回复样例Sample reply "第 2 句存在主谓一致错误：应为 The results suggest 而非 The results suggests。第 4 句的 however 用法不当，应改为 whereas 表示对比。以下是修改后的完整段落：……" "Sentence 2 has a subject–verb agreement error: use The results suggest, not The results suggests. In sentence 4, however is misused; replace it with whereas to signal contrast. Here is the fully revised paragraph: …"

条件 BCondition B

引导提示Guided scaffolding

只指出错误的位置与类型，以提问引导参与者自行修正，不给出正确形式。Flags only the location and type of each error and prompts the participant to correct it, without supplying the correct form.

回复样例Sample reply "你的第 2 句有一个主谓一致问题——主语是复数，检查一下动词。第 4 句有个连接词用错了：你想表达的是对比，这里用的词合适吗？改完后再发给我看看。" "Sentence 2 has a subject–verb agreement problem — the subject is plural, so check the verb. In sentence 4 a linker is misused: you mean to signal contrast, so is the word you chose right for that? Send it back when you have revised."
操纵检验Manipulation check 任务结束后以两题确认干预生效：① "AI 是否直接给出了正确写法？"（是/否）；② "你在修改时多大程度上需要自己思考？"（1–7）。若 A 组在①上多数答"否"，或两组在②上无差异，则视为操纵失败，需重新执行。 Two items confirm the intervention landed: (i) "Did the AI give you the correct wording directly?" (yes/no); (ii) "How much did you have to think for yourself while revising?" (1–7). If most of condition A answer "no" to (i), or the two conditions do not differ on (ii), the manipulation is treated as failed and the run is repeated.
05Measures

测什么，怎么测What we measure, and how

指标分三层：主要结局是学习者产出的客观质量，过程指标记录他实际做了什么，次要结局是任务后的主观评价。只有第一层能回答"有没有效果"，第二层回答"效果是怎么发生的"，第三层回答"体验如何"。 Measures fall into three tiers: primary outcomes are the objective quality of what the learner produced, process measures record what the learner actually did, and secondary outcomes are subjective ratings collected after the task. Only the first tier can answer "did it work"; the second explains how; the third describes how it felt.

06Analysis plan

每个 RQ 对应哪个检验Which test answers which RQ

分析方案在数据回收之前确定，避免看到结果后再挑检验方法。核心思路：主效应看差异，交互作用看调节，过程指标看机制，主客观一致性看效度。 The analysis plan is fixed before data collection, so that tests are not chosen after seeing the results. Main effects establish differences, the interaction tests moderation, process measures probe mechanism, and the subjective–objective check speaks to validity.

    存在"反馈方式 × 时间"交互作用：B 组在延迟迁移测验上优于 A 组，尽管其即时修订表现不占优。引导式反馈迫使学习者主动加工错误，形成的知识更持久——这正是"只发问卷"永远测不出的结论。 There is a feedback × time interaction: condition B outperforms A on the delayed transfer test, despite not leading on immediate revision. Guided feedback forces learners to process errors actively, producing more durable knowledge — a conclusion a questionnaire could never reach.
    检验：混合 ANOVA，交互项 F(1, —)，效应量 偏 η²；显著后做简单效应分析Test: mixed ANOVA, interaction F(1, —), effect size partial η²; simple effects if significant
  • H4 对应 RQ4 · 过程机制RQ4 · mechanism
    自主修正数与迁移表现正相关，而建议采纳率与迁移表现弱相关甚至负相关：全盘照收 AI 建议能快速改好当次稿子，却无助于长期掌握。 Self-corrections correlate positively with transfer, whereas adoption rate correlates weakly or even negatively: accepting AI suggestions wholesale fixes the draft at hand but does little for durable learning.
    检验：Pearson r + 多元回归（自主修正数、采纳率、轮次 → 迁移得分），报告 R²Test: Pearson r + multiple regression (self-corrections, adoption, turns → transfer), report R²
  • H5 对应 RQ5 · 一致性检验RQ5 · correspondence
    主观有用性评分与客观修订质量仅弱相关（r ≈ 0.2–0.3）。这一假设若成立，就直接证明：把问卷当作评估依据是无效的，必须测量真实的任务产出。 Subjective usefulness ratings correlate only weakly with objective revision quality (r ≈ 0.2–0.3). If supported, this directly demonstrates that questionnaires are invalid as sole evaluation evidence and that real task output must be measured.
    检验：Pearson r；同时报告两组在认知负荷上的差异（独立样本 t）Test: Pearson r; also report between-group cognitive-load difference (independent t)

09Reflection

这个设计撑不住什么What this design cannot carry

方法局限Methodological limitations 样本为便利抽样，预计集中在 B、C 两个核心班，A 班与 High Level 样本偏少；虽然按层级分层随机化可保证条件分配均衡，但样本本身不代表整体一年级。单次 35 分钟任务只覆盖一次写作—修订循环，无法判断长期使用后的效应衰减或累积。此外"延迟迁移"仅间隔 1 周，属于短期保持证据，不能推断学期尺度的学习成效。 The sample is one of convenience, likely concentrated in the two core groups B and C and thin in A and High Level; stratified randomisation balances condition assignment, but the sample is not representative of the Year 1 cohort. A single 35-minute session covers one writing–revision cycle only, so nothing can be said about decay or accumulation over sustained use. The transfer test sits just one week out — evidence of short-term retention, not of learning at the scale of a semester.
执行中预计会遇到的问题What we expect to go wrong
  1. 条件污染：同班参与者可能互相比较 AI 回复内容，从而猜到存在两种条件。拟将会谈安排在不同时段，并在任务后统一说明。Condition contamination — participants in the same class may compare AI replies and notice two conditions exist. Sessions will be scheduled apart and the design debriefed only after the task.
  2. 修订努力不均：B 组任务更难，参与者可能在时间未到时就放弃。拟记录放弃时刻并在分析时作为协变量检查。Unequal revision effort — condition B is harder, so participants may give up early. Time of disengagement will be logged and checked as a covariate.
  3. 评分者漂移：两名评分者在长时间评分后标准可能松动。拟随机化评分顺序并每隔 20 份做一致性复核。Rater drift — standards may loosen over a long scoring session. Order will be randomised and agreement re-checked every 20 scripts.
  4. 迁移测验流失：1 周后的回访预计有缺席。拟在招募时即说明两次参与，并对缺失数据做敏感性分析。Attrition at transfer — some participants will not return after a week. Two-session commitment is stated at recruitment, and missing data will be handled with a sensitivity analysis.
如果重做一次，我们会改什么What we would change next time
  1. 改用分层抽样，按四个 EAP 层级设配额，保证每组每层至少 15 人。Switch to stratified sampling with quotas across the four EAP levels, at least 15 per cell per level.
  2. 增加延迟后测时点（1 周 + 1 个月），区分短期保持与长期迁移。Add a later follow-up (1 week + 1 month) to separate short-term retention from long-term transfer.
  3. 加入无 AI 对照组，把"AI 反馈的效应"与"单纯重写一遍的效应"分离开。Add a no-AI control arm to separate the effect of AI feedback from the effect of simply rewriting once.
  4. 对关键错误做逐类编码（主谓一致、冠词、衔接等），定位哪类错误最能从引导式反馈中获益。Code errors by category (agreement, articles, cohesion, etc.) to identify which error types benefit most from guided feedback.
研究伦理Research ethics

所有参与者在任务开始前均阅读信息表并勾选同意；草稿与交互记录以编号存储，不含姓名、学号等标识符；参与者可随时退出且不影响课程成绩；两种反馈方式均不会造成学业劣势——条件 B 的参与者在任务结束后会收到完整解析，确保不留知识缺口。分析结果仅以汇总形式呈现，不回溯到个人。 All participants read the information sheet and tick consent before the task begins; drafts and interaction logs are stored under codes with no names or student IDs; participants may withdraw at any time without affecting their grades; neither condition creates academic disadvantage — participants in condition B receive a full explanation once the task ends, so no knowledge gap is left behind. Results are reported in aggregate only and are not traceable to individuals.
