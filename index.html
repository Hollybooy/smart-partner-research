"Smart Partner" 是一个面向西交利物浦大学大一学生的 AI 学习智能体，旨在帮助学生顺利通过 EAP（学术英语）考试。它以对话方式为学生提供个性化辅导，包括但不限于语法专项练习、写作批改反馈、词汇巩固与模拟测试等功能。本次调研将通过问卷与访谈，考察该智能体在一年级学生中的真实使用情况、使用目的及其对 EAP 学习的帮助程度。 "Smart Partner" is an AI learning agent designed for Year 1 students at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, aiming to help them pass their EAP (English for Academic Purposes) exams. Through conversational tutoring, it offers personalised support including — but not limited to — targeted grammar practice, writing feedback, vocabulary consolidation and mock tests. This study will survey how Year 1 students actually use the agent, why they use it, and how much it helps their EAP learning.

2目标人群Target AudienceAudience

调研对象构成（按 EAP 班级）Respondents by EAP group

调研对象为西浦大一各专业学生，覆盖 EAP 四个层级：A 为基础班，B、C 为核心班（学生人数最多），High Level 为高阶班。抽样同时兼顾文理工不同专业，以反映智能体在整体一年级群体中的使用图景。 Respondents are Year 1 students across all majors at XJTLU, covering the four EAP levels: A is the foundation stream, B and C are the core streams (where most students are placed), and High Level is the advanced stream. Sampling also spans arts, science and engineering programmes to reflect usage across the whole Year 1 cohort.

抽样方法Sampling method

本次采用便利抽样（convenience sampling）：通过各 EAP 班级群与年级群发放问卷链接，属于非概率抽样。选择它是因为大一学生规模大、且无法获得完整抽样框；代价是各层级样本量不均（A 班 26 人、High Level 37 人），因此我们在推断时补充了非参数检验，并在结论中明确标注这一局限。 We used convenience sampling: the questionnaire link was distributed through EAP class groups and year-group chats, a non-probability method. It was chosen because the Year 1 cohort is large and no complete sampling frame was available; the cost is uneven cell sizes across levels (26 in A, 37 in High Level), which is why non-parametric tests were added and the limitation is stated openly in our conclusions.

3调研设计Study DesignDesign

本研究为横断面调查（cross-sectional survey），在单一时间点采集使用情况与态度数据，属于非实验性设计——我们不对变量做操控，只测量自然状态下的差异与关联，因此所有结论均为相关而非因果。 This is a cross-sectional survey: usage and attitude data were collected at a single time point under a non-experimental design. No variable was manipulated — we only measure differences and associations as they occur naturally — so all findings are correlational rather than causal.

实验计划Experiment plan
  1. 阶段 1 · 背景调研：梳理 Smart Partner 的功能定位与 EAP 分班制度，明确研究问题。Stage 1 · Background — review Smart Partner's positioning and the EAP streaming system; fix the research questions.
  2. 阶段 2 · 工具设计：编制结构化问卷（封闭题 + 开放题）与半结构化访谈提纲。Stage 2 · Instrument design — build a structured questionnaire (closed + open items) and a semi-structured interview guide.
  3. 阶段 3 · 预测试：邀请 12 名大一学生试填，据此修改 3 处表述歧义，平均填答时间 6 分 20 秒。Stage 3 · Pilot — 12 Year 1 students trialled the questionnaire; three ambiguous wordings were revised and mean completion time was 6 min 20 s.
  4. 阶段 4 · 正式发放：通过班级群投放，两周内回收 260 份。Stage 4 · Fieldwork — distributed via class groups; 260 responses returned within two weeks.
  5. 阶段 5 · 数据清洗：剔除作答不完整或逻辑矛盾的 42 份，保留 218 份。Stage 5 · Cleaning — 42 incomplete or logically inconsistent responses were discarded, leaving 218.
  6. 阶段 6 · 统计分析：描述统计 → 假设检验（卡方、ANOVA、相关、Wilcoxon）。Stage 6 · Analysis — descriptive statistics, then inferential tests (chi-square, ANOVA, correlation, Wilcoxon).
  7. 阶段 7 · 呈现与反思：结果可视化并汇报，反思执行中的偏差与改进方向。Stage 7 · Reporting — visualise results, present, and reflect on bias and improvements.

为什么选这些评估方法Why these evaluation methods

方法选择遵循三条标准：① 变量的测量水平（分类变量用卡方，连续变量用 ANOVA / 相关）；② 实验设计（四组比较用单因素 ANOVA，前后测用配对检验）；③ 参数检验假设是否满足。自评信心为等级数据且未通过正态性检验，故改用 Wilcoxon 符号秩这一非参数替代；四个层级样本量不均衡，因此同时报告效应量（Cramér's V、η²），而不只看 p 值。 Methods were chosen on three criteria: (i) measurement level (chi-square for categorical, ANOVA/correlation for scale); (ii) design (one-way ANOVA for four groups, paired tests for pre/post); and (iii) whether parametric assumptions hold. Self-rated confidence is ordinal and failed the normality check, so the Wilcoxon signed-rank test was used instead; because cell sizes are uneven across the four levels, effect sizes (Cramér's V, η²) are reported alongside p values.

Users averaged 3.32 sessions a week (SD 1.33) of about 24 minutes, and 78.9% reported using it more in the week before an exam. By level, the foundation group A uses it most (84.6% adoption, 4.2 sessions a week) and High Level least (48.6%, 2.1), with the two core groups B and C in between and close to each other (70.3% / 67.9%) — the students who most need to catch up are the ones leaning on it hardest. Grammar practice (74%) and writing feedback (68%) dominate, while revision planning (29%) is used least: students treat it as a practice-and-correction tool rather than a study-planning tool.

关键发现Key finding 使用目的高度集中在应试导向：82% 的使用者首要目的是"顺利通过 EAP 考试"，其次是提高语法准确性（63%）与提升学术写作（58%）；而以"培养自主学习习惯"为目的的仅 37%。这说明 Smart Partner 目前被定位为考前冲刺工具，其长期学习支持价值尚未被充分使用。 Motivation is strongly exam-oriented: 82% of users say their main purpose is passing the EAP exam, followed by grammar accuracy (63%) and academic writing (58%); only 37% use it to build study habits. 7统计分析Statistical TestsTests

数据清洗后（剔除 42 份作答不完整或逻辑矛盾的问卷），我们按数据的实际情况逐条确定检验方法：先看变量类型与测量水平，再看组间/组内设计与条件数，最后检查参数检验的前假设（Shapiro–Wilk 正态性、Levene 方差齐性）。假设不满足时，改用对应的非参数检验作为替代。所有检验均为双尾，显著性水平 α = 0.05。 After cleaning (42 incomplete or logically inconsistent responses were discarded), each test was matched to the shape of the data: variable type and measurement level first, then between-/within-group design and number of conditions, then the assumptions behind the parametric tests (Shapiro–Wilk normality, Levene homogeneity of variance). Where an assumption failed, the matching non-parametric test was used instead. All tests are two-tailed at α = 0.05.

8研究反思ReflectionReflection

方法局限Methodological limitations 本次为便利抽样，样本集中在 B、C 两个核心班（合计 155 人，占 71.1%，与真实人数分布一致），但基础班 A 仅 26 人、高阶班 High Level 37 人，各层级样本量不均衡会影响 ANOVA 的稳健性——这也是我们对等级变量补充非参数检验的原因。此外问卷为自陈式，使用次数与时长可能存在回忆偏差；后续可结合后台使用日志做交叉验证，并补访高阶班学生，弄清他们为什么用得最少。 This was a convenience sample, concentrated in the two core groups B and C (155 respondents, 71.1% — consistent with where students actually sit), but foundation group A contributed only 26 respondents and High Level only 37. The unbalanced cell sizes affect the robustness of the ANOVA, which is why non-parametric tests were also run on the ordinal items. The questionnaire is self-report, so frequency and duration are subject to recall bias; next we would cross-validate against server-side usage logs and interview High Level students to find out why they use it least.
执行过程中遇到的问题What went wrong in practice
  1. 层级分布不可控：便利抽样导致 A 班与 High Level 样本偏少，原先计划的"四组等额比较"无法实现，只能改为报告效应量并补充非参数检验。Uneven level sizes — convenience sampling left groups A and High Level thin, so the planned equal-cell comparison was impossible; we switched to reporting effect sizes and adding non-parametric tests.
  2. 预测试暴露表述歧义：初版"使用频率"一题有 3 名学生理解为"打开次数"而非"完整会话"，已改为按"次会话"定义并加注说明。Ambiguous wording — in the pilot, three students read "use frequency" as app opens rather than full sessions; the item was redefined as "sessions" with a clarifying note.
  3. 自陈数据偏差：部分学生把"考前一周"理解为"整个考试月"，导致时长估计偏高，因此我们在结论中只报告趋势而非精确值。Self-report bias — some students read "the week before the exam" as "the whole exam month", inflating duration estimates; we therefore report trends rather than exact values.
  4. 因果推断受限：横断面设计无法判断是"使用智能体提升了信心"，还是"本来更有信心的学生更愿意用"，故第 7 部分的相关结论明确标注为相关而非因果。No causal claim — a cross-sectional design cannot tell whether using the agent raised confidence or more confident students were keener to use it, so the correlation in Section 7 is flagged explicitly as non-causal.
如果重做一次，我们会改什么What we would change next time
  1. 改用分层抽样，按四个 EAP 层级设定配额，保证每组至少 50 人。Switch to stratified sampling with quotas across the four EAP levels, at least 50 per cell.
  2. 接入后台使用日志，用客观数据交叉验证自陈的频率与时长。Wire in server-side usage logs to cross-validate self-reported frequency and duration.
  3. 追加纵向设计：在学期初与期末各测一次信心，用配对检验替代横断面的相关分析。Add a longitudinal design: measure confidence at the start and end of term, replacing cross-sectional correlation with a paired test.
  4. 对 High Level 学生做专门访谈，解释他们使用率最低的原因。Run targeted interviews with High Level students to explain their lowest adoption.
研究伦理Research ethics

所有参与者在填答前均看到信息表并勾选同意；数据匿名化处理，不含姓名、学号等标识符；参与者可随时退出且不影响课程成绩。分析结果仅以汇总形式呈现，不回溯到个人。 All participants saw the information sheet and ticked consent before answering; data were anonymised with no names or student IDs, and participants could withdraw at any time without affecting their grades. Results are reported in aggregate only and are not traceable to individuals.
