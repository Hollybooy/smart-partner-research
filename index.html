"Smart Partner" 是一个面向西交利物浦大学大一学生的 AI 学习智能体，旨在帮助学生顺利通过 EAP（学术英语）考试。它以对话方式为学生提供个性化辅导，包括但不限于语法专项练习、写作批改反馈、词汇巩固与模拟测试等功能。本次调研通过问卷与访谈，考察该智能体在一年级学生中的真实使用情况、使用目的及其对 EAP 学习的帮助程度。 "Smart Partner" is an AI learning agent designed for Year 1 students at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, aiming to help them pass their EAP (English for Academic Purposes) exams. Through conversational tutoring, it offers personalised support including — but not limited to — targeted grammar practice, writing feedback, vocabulary consolidation and mock tests. This study surveys how Year 1 students actually use the agent, why they use it, and how much it helps their EAP learning.

2目标人群Target AudienceAudience

调研对象构成（按 EAP 班级）Respondents by EAP group

调研对象为西浦大一各专业学生，重点覆盖 EAP 课程在读人群。抽样兼顾不同 EAP 班级与文理工专业，以反映智能体在整体一年级群体中的使用图景。 Respondents are Year 1 students across all majors at XJTLU, with a focus on those currently taking EAP modules. Sampling covers different EAP groups and both arts and science schools to reflect usage across the whole Year 1 cohort.

关键发现Key finding 语法练习是 Smart Partner 被使用最多的功能——超过四分之三的使用者用它做语法专项训练，其次为写作批改反馈。 Grammar practice is the most-used feature — over three quarters of users practise grammar with Smart Partner, followed by writing feedback.

4使用目的PurposesPurposes

学生使用 Smart Partner 的目的占比Why students use Smart Partner

关键发现Key finding "通过 EAP 考试" 是绝对主导的使用目的；同时约半数学生希望借助智能体在长期上提升语法准确性与学术写作水平，说明它不仅被当作考前工具，也被视为日常学习伙伴。 "Passing the EAP exam" is the dominant purpose; meanwhile about half of students also aim to improve grammar accuracy and academic writing in the long run — the agent serves not only as a pre-exam tool but also as a daily study companion.
