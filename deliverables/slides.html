Study Design调研设计 · 横断面调查

A cross-sectional, non-experimental survey: usage and attitude data collected at one time point. No variable is manipulated, so findings are correlational rather than causal.

Experiment plan 实验计划

  2. Instrument design (questionnaire + interview guide)
  3. Pilot test with ~12 students; revise wording
  4. Fieldwork via class group chats
  5. Data cleaning & coding
  6. Descriptive then inferential analysis

Reflection & Next Steps研究反思与改进

What went wrong 执行中的问题

  • Convenience sampling left the foundation and advanced cells thin, so equal-cell comparison was impossible.
  • Pilot testing exposed ambiguous wording in the frequency item.
  • Self-reported frequency and duration carry recall bias.
  • A cross-sectional design cannot separate cause from effect.

What we would change 改进方向

  1. Stratified sampling with quotas per EAP level.
  2. Cross-validate against server-side usage logs.
  3. Add a longitudinal pre/post measure of confidence.
  4. Targeted interviews with High Level students.
Ethics. Participation was voluntary and anonymous, with an information sheet and consent form completed before every response; results are reported in aggregate only.
